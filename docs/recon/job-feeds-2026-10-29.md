## Honest read
- **Few Indian sources are both machine-readable and allowed.**
  - Most Indian hiring runs on Naukri, LinkedIn, WhatsApp forwards, campus drives and the big recruiters' own portals.
  - Those are closed, block bots, or forbid automated reading in their terms (Naukri clause 17).
  - So a feed is the only way to get bulk volume legally, and it costs money.
- **The cheapest legal volume is jobs the student or placement officer brings to us:**
  - **Paste a job** (and later "share to Hirelane" from the Naukri or LinkedIn app on Android)
  - the **drive inbox**
  - These cover every site without us reading any of them.
- **Feeds dilute the "honest data" promise.** Aggregator listings can be stale or duplicated. Any feed job must show "via <feed>", be dropped if older than 30 days, and still go through scam flags.

Sources: developer.adzuna.com/overview and /docs/terms_of_service; jobspipe.dev blog posts on the Careerjet and Jooble APIs (a competitor's blog, so treat with care).
