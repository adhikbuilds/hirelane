# Superset teardown (recon 2026-10-29)

Sources: joinsuperset.com home page and the Launchpad blog post (joinsuperset.com/blogs/superset-launchpad-navigating-your-path-to-freshers-job-opportunities/), both read 2026-10-29. Also listing-site reviews (Capterra, G2, GetApp) and a placementpilot.ai comparison, marked [relayed]. Public pages only. No account was created and no data was copied. Superset is a reference we study, not something we copy: never use its name, product names, copy or data in the product.

## Product
India's incumbent campus-hiring platform; formerly TnpSuite (Business Standard, 2018). Three sides:
- **Placement cell:**
  - **TnpSuite** is "the institution's system of record": student data and eligibility, job postings, applications, shortlists, interview rounds, offers.
  - Also: drive scheduling, fraud detection, an employer CRM, online career fairs, virtual pool campuses and internship programmes.
- **Students:**
  - **Launchpad+**: resume intelligence, InterviewEdge AI mock interviews, placement stories from past batches, assessment bundles, learning cohorts and counselling.
  - Open sign-up, "no college onboarding required". One profile covers campus placements, competitions and experienced roles. Android and iOS apps.
  - The Launchpad blog describes partner-company jobs (100+ hiring partners) unlocked by clearing one Superset assessment instead of separate company tests; interviews "within a couple of weeks".
- **Employers:**
  - campus hiring automation, plus built-in tests: cognitive, technical, behavioural, AI English evaluation, work simulations, proctoring
  - live coding interviews, AI interviews (AVA) and AI resume shortlisting (TalentLens)
  - employer branding and campaigns
  - experienced hiring

## Scale and pricing
- Scale [site]: 1,400+ institutions, 30M+ students, 600+ employers. Outreach to 24,000 campuses.
- Older counts [relayed]: 115+ paying colleges (2018); 600+ and 750 also appear.
- Pricing: "talk to sales", no list price. Described as out of reach for most tier-2/3 colleges [relayed: placementpilot.ai].

## What it does well (learn from)
- **Owns the TPO's system of record.** Eligibility rules, drives and offers all live in it, so the placement cell's daily work happens there. Switching cost is high.
- **One assessment across many employers.** It removes repeated tests for students and gives employers a pre-screened pool. That is a real network effect.
- **Student sign-up without the college.** It builds the student network directly, as Handshake does in the US.
- **Vocabulary to reuse in our TPO copy:** drives, eligibility, shortlists, rounds, offers, pool campus.

## Gaps (where Hirelane fits)
- **Supply is partner employers only** ("100+ hiring partners" for Launchpad). Student reviews complain that jobs are not frequent enough [relayed: Capterra]. Hirelane reads thousands of employer career pages.
- **No off-campus outcome reporting.** Its site doesn't mention NAAC or NIRF, yet colleges file off-campus offers for NAAC 5.2.1 by hand.
- **Little help for students outside drives.** Eligibility on open-market roles, scam checks on jobs found elsewhere, and an unreadable-resume check are not visible on its pages.
- Tier-2/3 affordability [relayed].

## Strategy implications
- **Don't build a TPO ERP.** Import the cohort from it (CSV: roll number, programme, passout year, placed yes/no). Placed students drop out of picks.
- **Position as the after-the-drive layer:**
  - open-market roles
  - honest academic eligibility
  - scam shield
  - resume health
  - consented off-campus outcome export
- **Watch Launchpad+.** It is the closest student-side overlap. If it opens to open-market jobs, our supply edge narrows to freshness, eligibility honesty and outcomes.
