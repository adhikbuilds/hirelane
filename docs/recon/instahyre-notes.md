# Instahyre (recon 2026-10-16; patterns only)

Source: the public candidate help centre (https://help.instahyre.com/en/category/instahyre-for-candidates-rhe292/).
The main site returns 403 to automated fetches, so it was not scraped.

**Candidate loop:**
- Sign up with email, Gmail or LinkedIn.
- "Add your skills": fresher or professional, years of experience, role, skills.
- **Opportunities** tab: roles matched to your resume and preferences, with Save, Apply or Not interested. "Not
  interested" feeds the matcher ("Instamatch"). View opens details and Apply.
- **Activities:** tracks your applications. Applications can't be withdrawn once sent.
- **Recruiters Activities:** what recruiters did with your profile.
- **Profile and Job preferences:** desired locations, current salary (LPA), notice period, and job-search status
  (set "not looking" to pause recommendations).
- **"Got Instahyred?":** claim a joining bonus or gift box after being hired.

**Borrow for Hirelane:**
- skip with a reason, which visibly retrains matches
- Indian-market preferences (fresher, LPA, notice period, job-search status)
- a "you got hired" celebration and share card

We have no recruiter side (we read public ATS boards), so recruiter activity becomes **replies in your inbox**.
