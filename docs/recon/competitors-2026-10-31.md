# Competitor feature depth (recon 2026-10-31)

These notes come from public pages read with a fetch tool. Every vendor claim is unverified. No account was created and nothing was copied: this is a reference for study, never for our copy, assets or names. Unstop's home page returned a cookie wall, so the Unstop notes come from search results. See superset-notes.md for the 10-29 overview.

## Superset: placement cell (TnpSuite) — joinsuperset.com/university
- **System of record:** student data and eligibility, job postings, applications, shortlists, rounds and offers in one cycle view. The vendor claims +22% conversions and 80% less tracking time.
- **Per-drive eligibility rules,** e.g. "CGPA ≥ 7.0, no active backlogs" → 1,246 eligible. Lists refresh automatically when records change, and students are notified of eligibility.
- **Drive funnel counts:** eligible 1,246 → applied 214 → cleared test 86 → interviewed 32 → offers 9.
- **Departments verify profiles** (marks, backlogs), which carry a "Records verified by the college" badge.
- **Assessments Builder:** question banks, papers with sections and negative marking (+3/−1), schedules, results and proctored sittings.
- **Drive-day control centre:** sequences rounds, panels, rooms and slots. Students book interview slots, and the centre shows live status per panel.
- **Approval chains:** offers and bulk rollouts need sign-off before release.
- **Reminders** go to eligible students who haven't applied.
- **Live activity feed:** offers, shortlists, eligibility changes, verifications.
- **Internships:** allotment, work logs and evaluations.
- **Virtual fairs and pool-campus events.**
- **Resumes** are generated in the college's own format from verified records (PDF). Profiles can be shared by link, with employer views tracked.
- **Security:** SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, SSO, MFA, and RBAC scoped per team, role and drive. "Custom integrations".
- **Not stated:** NAAC/NIRF reports, a pricing list, notification channels, a named integration list.

## Superset: placement-cell employer CRM — joinsuperset.com/university-crm
- **Records:** shared company and contact records synced with TnpSuite. Spreadsheet import, relationship owners, and filters (e.g. "hired here before"). Contacts carry branches, visit years and custom fields.
- **Season pipeline:** Lead 10% → In conversation 30% → Invitation sent 45% → Registered 70% → JD posted 90% → Drive done 100%. Stages can be reordered. Three stages advance automatically from portal events. Relationships carry over between seasons ("31 of 48 carried over").
- **Invitations:** on letterhead, with per-recipient status (96 accepted / 88 awaiting / 21 declined / 7 undelivered of 212) and weekly response tracking.
- **Broadcasts:** visual or HTML editor, personalisation, brochure attachments, scheduling, open and click tracking, and automatic unsubscribe skipping.
- **Email:** sends via Gmail, Outlook, SMTP or their own domain. Unified inbox, a BCC dropbox that logs mail to a deal, and replies saved to the deal.
- **Deals:** an owner, a win probability and an activity timeline. Tasks have due dates. Student coordinators request access to a deal and the owner approves.
- **Analytics:** a season dashboard by campaign, coordinator and date, per-coordinator activity, .xlsx export and RBAC.
- **AI:** employer recommendations, alumni connections, outreach drafts, reply-intent classification, opportunity scoring, meeting notes → tasks, and "Ask the CRM".

## Superset: student (Launchpad+) — joinsuperset.com/student
- **Profile and resume:** college-verified profile (CGPA badge), one-page templates, PDF export or a live share link.
- **"Recommended for you"** shows a % match that names the matching skills, refreshed daily, with an Apply/Register button. Stretch roles are flagged "Prepare first". The site claims 4,000+ verified jobs per month.
- **One profile** covers campus drives, hackathons and lateral roles. Each application shows its stage (registered, OA, interview, accepted).
- **Practice:** InterviewEdge AI mock interviews (clarity, depth, fluency, structure); Eloquence English score out of 10; a readiness diagnostic with strong and focus areas plus pathways; assessment bundles; learning cohorts; counselling; moderated placement stories from past batches.
- **Apps:** Android and iOS. Notifications for test slots, interview dates and status.

## Superset: student complaints (App Store, Play, Capterra; search results 10-31)
- Push notifications are missed ("missed important updates"), and tapping a notification crashes the app (Dec 2025).
- The app hangs since Launchpad was added. Inside the app, an opened PDF has no back button.
- **Eligibility errors:** "not eligible" appears even when the JD matches, and "your college is not eligible" shows on most jobs.
- The face check rejects profile photos for a month. Profile edits get stuck with no support reply, which blocks registration.
- A video interview's echo meant a student missed the round.
- **Admin reviews:** the mobile app lags. They want customizable reports and say it's hard to track many students at once.

## Handshake (US) — joinhandshake.com/career-centers
- The marketing page is shallow. It claims tracking "student engagement to post-grad outcomes". Employers post free. It runs virtual and in-person events and fairs, and sends curated jobs and events to students' phones.
- Its case studies cite outcome rates (99% at U Dallas) and appointment growth (+29% at Baylor). Appointments, SIS/SSO and first-destination detail are not on the public page.
- **Network model:** career-center adoption, then students, then employer premium.

## Unstop (search results; the home page is behind a cookie wall)
- **For colleges and societies:** free event listing with a microsite, payment collection, reminder emails, proctoring, submissions, certificates and merch. TPO partnerships focus on upskilling plus hiring exposure. There is no placement-management dashboard for TPOs.
- **For employers:** sourcing, AI-match screening, online assessments, virtual interviews and offers. A campaign dashboard shows gender diversity, college-wise engagement and application insights. 360° proctoring includes Aadhaar verification, plus ATS/HRIS integration. Campaigns run all year.
- **Scale claims:** 31M+ students, 800+ brands, 20k–42k colleges.

## Naukri Campus (search results)
- **Launch:** April 2024, a student-only platform with 1.5M students from about 20k colleges at launch. It calls itself "India's largest job and internship platform for freshers".
- **NCAT:** a free national aptitude test (quant, reasoning, verbal; 60 min) that mirrors TCS/Infosys/Wipro/Accenture placement tests. It gives certificates and ₹10L in prizes, and 99th percentile and above unlocks paid internships. It is positioned explicitly for tier-2/3 students with few campus drives. Free practice tests allow multiple attempts.
- No placement-cell tool was found.
