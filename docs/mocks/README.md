**Rules learned:**
- Keep the Wrapped look of the good rounds: bold colour blocks, giant Bricolage type, stamps, tilted cards, the story
  bars and the recap cards.
- Photos stay small and supporting, as in the good swipe mock, never full-bleed photo-led layouts.
- Interaction detail is shown as states (drag, sheet, toast, pressed) on the approved screens.
