# Target colleges for the first 10–20 conversations

**How to use this list:** contact a college through a student or alumnus there first (see `campus-validation-interns.md`), and only cold-email if you have nobody. Check each college's current placement contact on its own website before reaching out.

These are big multi-programme private universities with active placement cells. They expect polish and pay more, but decide slowly.

| # | University | City | Why it fits |

| 10 | Sharda University | Greater Noida | Mid-tier private, NCR |

## Tier-2/3 colleges (10 slots)
Choose specific colleges from the clusters below. They are affiliated to universities whose CGPA rules we model, so our eligibility maths matters there. Prefer colleges with B.Com/BBA or engineering batches of 200+ and a NAAC cycle due in the next 1–2 years.

| # | Cluster (affiliating university) | City | How to pick colleges |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11–12 | RGPV (engineering) / DAVV (commerce) | Indore, Bhopal | Matches the "Sample University, Indore" story in the mocks. Use the university's affiliated-college list. |
| 13–14 | AKTU | Lucknow, Ghaziabad | Large private engineering colleges. AKTU's CGPA formula is being fixed now. |
| 15–16 | VTU | Bengaluru, Mysuru | Students on the 2022 scheme graduate in 2026, our scheme-aware rule. |
| 17 | SPPU | Pune | Large commerce and engineering colleges, plus Mumbai-style rule questions. |
| 18 | RTMNU | Nagpur | Tier-2 city, many affiliated colleges |
| 19 | JNTUH | Hyderabad | A large affiliated-engineering cluster |
| 20 | Gujarat Technological University | Ahmedabad | A large affiliated-engineering cluster |

**Where to find specific colleges:**
- the university's own list of affiliated colleges
- the NAAC accredited-institutions list (look for a cycle due soon)
- your own and your interns' networks
