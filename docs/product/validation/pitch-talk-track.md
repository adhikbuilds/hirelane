# Pitch talk track: placement officer, 2 minutes

## Opening (20 seconds)
"Thank you for the time. I'm [name], building Hirelane here in Bengaluru. I'm not here to replace anything you use. I want to learn how you handle students who aren't placed on campus, and show you one thing that may save you time at NAAC."

## Ask before you show (40 seconds)
- "Roughly how many of your final years get placed through campus drives?"
- "When a student gets an off-campus job or joins higher studies, how do you find out?"
- "Who built the 5.2.1 list last time, and how long did it take?"

Let them talk. Write down numbers and exact words.

## Show (45 seconds, demo as placement officer)
1. **Overview:** "This is your batch. 34 off-campus offers, 22 verified by you, and what to act on this week."
2. **Outcomes:** "Students upload their offer or admission letter. You verify it here, and every view is logged."
3. **NAAC 5.2.1 download:** "One click gives you the list, with year, programme, employer, package and higher studies."
4. Optional, if they're interested: switch to the student demo. "Students only see roles they qualify for, with the maths: '12th: 58%, needs 60%'."

## Close (15 seconds)
"Would you try it free with one programme for one semester? We'd agree the success measure with you at the start. Who else would need to say yes?"

## Likely objections
| They say | You answer |
|---|---|
| "We already use Superset / our ERP." | "Keep it. Hirelane sits after the drives, for students those tools don't place. You import a roster CSV from any export." |
| "Student data privacy?" | "Students choose what you see. By default it's totals only, and groups under 10 are hidden. We keep a one-way hash of each email, not names or roll numbers, and students can delete everything." |
| "What does it cost?" | "The pilot is free. We agree pricing only after the pilot meets the success measure we set together." Don't quote a number. |
| "Students won't use another app." | "It's a website, nothing to install. It shows only roles they qualify for and warns them about scam posts. That's what makes them come back." |
| "Is your NAAC format right?" | "It follows the 5.2.1 placement and higher-studies data template. Tell me your IQAC's format and we'll match it." |
| "Does it apply on students' behalf?" | "Not during the pilot. Students apply on the company's own site with answers ready. Automatic applying stays off until it's approved." |
| "Who are you? Will you be around?" | "A small Bengaluru team. That's why the pilot is free and small: you see it work before committing." |
