# Bengaluru colleges: placement contacts for the first wave (gathered 6 Nov 2026)

These are institutional contacts each college publishes on its own placement or contact page, for companies and partners. Before sending, open the source page and check the details are current. Search snippets can lag behind the pages. Use them for professional outreach only: one polite email, then one follow-up.

**Priority:** the best fit is colleges with large commerce/BBA/BCA batches and many students placed off campus (more NAAC pain). Top engineering colleges place well on campus; use them as references, not first pilots.
