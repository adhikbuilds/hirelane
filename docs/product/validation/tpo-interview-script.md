# Placement officer interview script

**Goal:** find out whether NAAC 5.2.1 outcome reporting is painful enough to pay for, before building more. Talk to 10 placement officers (TPOs) or IQAC coordinators. Aim for a mix: 3–4 large private universities and 6–7 tier-2/3 colleges.

**Rules for the interviewer:**
- Ask about the past, not the future ("last time you…", not "would you…").
- Don't pitch until the last section. Listen for numbers: hours, people, rupees, dates.
- Write down their exact words for anything painful.
- 30 minutes. Ask permission before recording.

## 1. Context (5 min)
1. What's your role, and how many final-year students does your cell handle each year?
2. Which tools do you use for placements today: an ERP, Superset, spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups?
3. Roughly what share of your final years get placed through on-campus drives?

## 2. Off-campus students (7 min)
4. What happens to students who aren't placed on campus? Who helps them?
5. How do you find out when a student gets an off-campus job or joins higher studies?
6. Tell me about the last time you had to chase students for offer letters. How long did it take?

## 3. NAAC and NIRF reporting (10 min)
7. When was your last NAAC self-study report? Who prepared criterion 5.2 (placement and progression)?
8. Walk me through how you built the 5.2.1 list: where the data came from, who collected it, how you verified it.
9. How many people-days did that take? What went wrong or was missing?
10. Has a peer team or reviewer ever questioned your placement numbers or evidence?

## 4. Money and decisions (5 min)
11. Have you paid for any placement or student tool in the last two years? Who approved it, and when in the year?
12. If something saved that 5.2.1 effort, whose budget would it come from?

## 5. Show and ask (3 min)
Show the mocks (console overview and the Outcomes and NAAC page) and the one-page offer.

13. What's the first thing you'd change?
14. Would you run a free one-semester pilot with one programme? Who else would need to agree?
