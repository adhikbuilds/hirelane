# Off-campus outcomes for NAAC 5.2.1

**For:** placement cells and IQAC coordinators.

## The problem
Students who aren't placed on campus still find jobs and join higher studies. The college rarely hears about it in time. When the NAAC self-study report is due, someone spends days chasing offer letters on WhatsApp and building the 5.2.1 list by hand, and the evidence is still patchy.

## What Hirelane does
- **Students report their own outcomes.** They upload an offer letter or an admission letter in the Hirelane app. It's free for them.
- **Your team verifies each one.** Open the letter, mark it verified, or ask the student for more. Every view is logged, and the student can see who opened it.
- **You download a 5.2.1-ready list.** It has year, programme, employer, package and higher-studies rows, verified rows only.
- **Your students get more chances along the way.** Off-campus roles checked against their marks, resume fixes, and scam warnings on jobs they find elsewhere.

## Privacy by design
- Students choose what you see. By default it's totals only, and any group under 10 is hidden.
- Only students who choose to share outcomes appear by name.
- We keep a one-way hash of each roster email, never names or roll numbers. The roster file is deleted after import.
- Students can delete their account; their data is gone within 24 hours.

## Works next to what you have
It doesn't replace your ERP or placement portal. Import a roster CSV from any export, and keep running campus drives the way you do today.
