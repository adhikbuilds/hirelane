# Where to find interns and placement officers

Contact communities and organisations, not lists of private individuals. Read each community's rules before posting; many subreddits ban self-promotion, so post as research or a paid internship, never as an ad.

## 1. TPO associations: the fastest route to placement officers
These groups bring hundreds of TPOs together and run conferences. One yes from an organiser can open 10 conversations. Member counts are each group's own claims.

| Group | Reach (self-reported) | How to approach | Site |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATPO India (national) | TPO meets, workshops, job fairs | Email the organisers: ask to speak at a TPO meet, or to survey members about NAAC 5.2.1 effort | https://atpoindia.in/ |
| TTPOA, Telangana | 379 TPOs from 214 colleges | Ask to join or sponsor an event; their events page lists conferences in Hyderabad | https://www.ttpoa.in/ |
| TTPOC, Telangana TPO Consortium | TPO network; "Career Nexus" events | Same approach as TTPOA | https://www.ttpoconsortium.com/ |
| TNATPO, Tamil Nadu and Puducherry | 843+ TPOs, 956+ institutions | Membership and co-branded summits are listed on their site | https://tnatpo.org/ |
| National TPO Club (Testbook) | Online TPO community | The page may be old; check it's still active | https://testbook.com/promos/nationaltpoclub.html |

**Email to an association (fill the brackets):**
> Subject: 20-minute input from TPOs on NAAC 5.2.1 reporting
>
> Dear [organiser name], I'm [your name], founder of Hirelane, a small startup building a free tool that helps students who aren't placed on campus find roles they qualify for, and helps placement cells verify off-campus and higher-studies outcomes for NAAC 5.2.1. Before building more, I want to learn how TPOs handle this today. Could I speak to a few of your members, or run a short session at an upcoming meet? Nothing to buy; I'll share what I learn with the association. [phone, email]

## 2. Reddit: for student interns and student pain, not TPOs
Communities to check (verify each exists and read its rules first):
- **r/developersIndia**: large, but focused on tech careers
- **r/Btechtards**: engineering students, with frequent tier-3 placement threads
- **r/Indian_Academia**: higher education in India
- Your own city or college subreddits, for example r/pune and r/bangalore (they often allow internship posts in weekly threads)

**Post text** (an internship, labelled clearly):
> **[Paid, remote] Campus research internship: talk to your placement cell about off-campus placements and NAAC**
> I'm building Hirelane, a free tool for final-year students who don't get placed on campus. I'm looking for student placement coordinators or reps at any college to set up a 30-minute chat with their placement officer and write a short summary. Paid per completed conversation. Comment or DM if you work with your placement cell. Mods: happy to remove this if it's not allowed.

**Also useful:** search these subreddits for "placement cell", "off campus" and "not eligible". Students' exact words make good evidence for the pitch.

## 3. Other platforms
| Platform | Use it for | How |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | TPOs directly, and student coordinators | Search "Training and Placement Officer" + city, or "placement coordinator" + college. Send a short note (the TPO intro in `campus-validation-interns.md`). Engage with TPO association posts. |
| Internshala | Interns | Post the listing from `campus-validation-interns.md` (paid per conversation) |
| Unstop | Interns, campus-ambassador style | List it as an internship |
| College E-cells and placement committees | Interns plus a warm path to the TPO | Email the E-cell; they like real startup work |
| WhatsApp/Telegram groups | Your own alumni and junior groups | Ask for one placement coordinator per college |

## Order to try
1. TPO association emails (ATPO, TTPOA, TNATPO) on day 1.
2. LinkedIn notes to 10–15 TPOs in your target clusters (see `target-colleges.md`).
3. The Internshala listing plus 2–3 subreddit posts, for interns.
4. Track everything in the table in `tpo-interview-script.md`.
