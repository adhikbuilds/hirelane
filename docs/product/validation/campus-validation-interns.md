# Campus validation interns

**Goal:** 10 placement officer conversations in about 3 weeks. Each intern opens the door at their own college (and one or two nearby colleges) and helps run the interview in `tpo-interview-script.md`.

## Who to look for
- Final-year or pre-final-year students at the colleges you want: a mix of large private universities and tier-2/3 colleges.
- Best fits:
  - **placement coordinators or student placement representatives**: they already work with the TPO every week
  - E-cell or entrepreneurship club leads
  - students who ran a college fest or a society
- Must be able to visit the placement cell in person, take careful notes, and write a short summary the same day.

## Where to find them
| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| Internshala | Post a part-time, remote "Campus research intern" listing (text below). It's the largest student internship site in India. |
| LinkedIn | Post from your own profile, and search "placement coordinator" and "training and placement cell" + college name to message student coordinators directly. |
| Unstop | List it as an internship or a campus-ambassador opportunity. |
| Your own network | Ask friends, juniors and alumni groups at target colleges for one placement coordinator each. This is often the fastest channel. |
| College E-cells | Email the E-cell or entrepreneurship club; they like real startup projects. |

## Internship posting (copy, then fill the brackets)

**Campus research intern, part-time, remote (4 weeks)**

Hirelane is building free tools that help final-year students find off-campus jobs they actually qualify for, and help placement cells report outcomes for NAAC.

We're looking for students who work with their college's placement cell to help us understand how placement officers handle off-campus students and NAAC reporting.

What you'll do:
- Set up a 30-minute conversation with your college's placement officer (and, if possible, one nearby college)
- Join the conversation, take notes using our script, and write a one-page summary
- Share what students in your college actually struggle with when applying off campus

What you'll get:
- [Stipend: ₹____ fixed, plus ₹____ for each completed and written-up conversation]
- A certificate and a letter of recommendation
- Direct work with the founder on a real product

Who should apply: final or pre-final year students who are placement coordinators, student representatives, or active in their college's E-cell. Any degree.

Time: about 3–5 hours a week for 4 weeks.

## Message to a student coordinator (LinkedIn or WhatsApp)
> Hi [name], I'm [your name], building Hirelane, a free tool that helps final-year students find off-campus roles they qualify for and helps placement cells report outcomes for NAAC. I'm looking for a few student coordinators to help me speak with their placement officer for 30 minutes, as a paid short internship. Would you be open to a quick call this week?

## Intro note the intern forwards to their TPO
> Dear Sir/Madam, I'm helping a small startup, Hirelane, understand how placement cells handle students who are placed off campus, and how outcomes are reported for NAAC 5.2.1. Could they have 30 minutes with you this week or next? There's nothing to buy; they want to learn how you work today. [Your name, phone, email]

## Ground rules for interns
- Interns introduce themselves as Hirelane research interns, never as college staff, and say plainly that Hirelane is a startup.
- Ask the TPO before recording. Without a yes, take notes only.
- Collect no student personal data. These conversations are about process, not students.
- No promises about prices, features or dates. Pass those questions to you.
- Write the summary into the results table in `tpo-interview-script.md` within 24 hours.

## Paying and tracking
- Pay a small fixed stipend plus a per-conversation amount, released once you receive the written summary.
- Track every intern in one sheet: name, college, TPO contacted (date), meeting held (date), summary received, paid.
