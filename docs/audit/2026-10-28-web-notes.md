# Platform web notes, 2026-10-28

What changed since docs/product/00-PLATFORMS.md (2026-10-19). Fetched today from each vendor's own homepage. Claims are the vendors', not checked.

## Tsenta (tsenta.com), YC
Flow, as the site describes it:
1. **Onboarding:** profile from the resume plus background. Preferences: location, salary, experience level, role family. Work-authorisation details are kept for application questions.
2. **Matching:** watches career pages on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and others (the hero says "30+ more ATSes", the apply section "51 more"). Matches get a % score and an explanation. You can paste any job URL to add a role by hand.
3. **Tailoring:** a keyword-aligned resume and cover letter per role. Headline: "Review every change before it goes out." You approve each change before it is sent.
4. **Submission:** logs into the employer's ATS, fills every field, answers open questions and uploads the resume. You can watch or leave it running. Each application produces a **receipt** listing the fields and answers submitted.
5. **Inbox:** connecting email is optional. It reads job mail, updates the tracker and drafts recruiter replies in your Drafts folder. "Tsenta does not send mail from your account on its own."
6. **Tracker:** stages Applied → Viewed → Replied → Interview.

- **Channels:** web, iOS, Android, iMessage, WhatsApp, a Chrome autofill extension, and an MCP server plus CLI for agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenClaw).
- **Pricing:** $19 / $39 / $99 a month for 600 / 1,500 / 4,500 applications per 30 days. The first 25 are free and you pay only for submitted applications. "Tiers differ only by volume."
- **FAQ:** no promise of sponsorship, and no promise that use is undetectable.

## Shortlist (shortlistapp.co)
The homepage was trimmed down, and the copy now stresses consent:
- "The person approves every send; nothing goes out without their OK."
- "Start free: 10 applications, no card."
- **Autopilot:** no longer mentioned on the homepage. The 10-19 notes had autopilot at ₹79/week. *Verify on /pricing before relying on either version.*
- **Public board:** 15,000+ roles, refreshed every 6h. Each role carries a parsed annual salary, named skills and a freshness indicator. There's a public JSON API (api.shortlistapp.co/public/jobs), a sitemap and an llms.txt.
- **Not covered on the page:** freshers, campus, replies and tracking. Based in Bengaluru.

## Jobright (jobright.ai)
- **Speed claim:** "matched jobs, autofill applications, tailored resume, and recommended insider connections in less than 1 min".
- **Matching:** "jobs you're truly qualified for … no fake listings and early alerts".
- **Resume tailoring:** a tailored resume per job; autofill "across all major ATS platforms", "hundreds of jobs daily".
- **Insider connections:** finds alumni and hiring managers; claims "4X" more interviews.
- **Orion:** a 24/7 AI career copilot, including interview prep.
- **Also listed:** a tracker (under related tools) and an employer side.
- **Claimed scale:** 3M users, 8M+ jobs, 400k+ new jobs a day.
- No seeker price on the homepage.

## Simplify (simplify.jobs)
- **Positioning:** "a personal AI talent agent for job seekers".
- **Matching:** matches plus curated new-grad, internship and startup lists.
- **Resume tools:** resume tailoring against the job description, and a resume builder.
- **Copilot extension:** autofill on "100,000+ company career sites in one click".
- **Tracker:** one dashboard for applications, interviews and offers.
- **Talent agent:** introduces candidates to hiring teams (an employer-side marketplace).
- **Audience:** students and new grads first, then experienced and remote seekers.
- No price on the homepage.

## Hallmark (usehallmark.com): a tool, not a competitor

- **Installed at:** ~/hirelane/.claude/skills/hallmark on 2026-10-28.

  - no eyebrows or tracked caps
  - no invented metrics

  - about 80 words per screen
  - focus-visible states and a reduced-motion fallback
